From Systems Engineer to Streaming Infrastructure Leader

My career has always been a journey across the infrastructure spectrum. I have focused on the infrastructure most people never see, but rely on constantly. Here's the path that got me here.

1999 – 2007 · Sytec Business Solutions

Sr. Systems Engineer / Technical Services Director

Started my career supporting business IT systems for clients across industries, learning the fundamentals of networks, servers, and client-facing technical leadership from the ground up.

2007 – 2016 · MTM Technologies

Sr. Systems Consultant

Spent nine years designing and implementing enterprise infrastructure for clients, deepening my focus on network architecture expertise and building on my technical experience.

2016 – 2020 · MLB Advanced Media & BAMTECH Media

Sr. Network Architect

Joined the network team at MLB, building the streaming technology behind MLB At Bat. The team ran the engine behind ESPN+, Disney+, and other major streaming platforms. I focus on network architecture to deliver live video at scale.

2020 – 2024 · The Walt Disney Company

Senior Manager, Network Engineering

Stepped into leadership, managing the engineering team responsible for the network infrastructure behind Disney's streaming platforms.

2024 – Present · The Walt Disney Company

Director, Network Engineering — Disney Entertainment & ESPN Technology

Lead network engineering strategy for the systems that carry encoding and content delivery for Disney's streaming services, from origin to viewer, reliably and at scale.

2023 – Present · Texas Tech University

Finishing My Degree

I am back in school to complete my bachelor's degree, finishing a journey I started nearly 30 years ago.

Skills & Interests

Network Architecture

Nearly two decades designing network infrastructure for scale, reliability, and performance.

Streaming & CDN Infrastructure

Encoding and content delivery systems that move video from origin to viewer.

Technical Leadership

Leading and growing engineering teams, from individual contributor to director.
